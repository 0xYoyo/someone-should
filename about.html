how

  1. wishes harvested from hacker news, software recommendations stack exchange and reddit — — candidates, one line each, linked, no usernames.
  2. ranked by agreement: votes plus comments, summed across duplicates.
  3. the top 100 built as single-file tools by opus 5.5 subagents, one per wish, under a verify gate: zero network requests, loads in under a second on a phone, three behaviour tests, 360 and 390 px viewports. failures are dropped, not shipped.
  4. he approves what ships. nothing unapproved is marked built.
